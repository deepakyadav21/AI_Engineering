Evaluating Delivery Strategies: A Simulation Study for We-Doo
Module Name: Modelling, Simulation and Optimisation

This research presents a simulation study evaluating daily parcel delivery strategies for the 'We-Doo' delivery service company. The customer location data used in this study was generated based on the last four digits of the student identification number, resulting in a map with 100 customer locations.

The simulation covers a period of 50 days of delivery to analyze the effectiveness of the proposed strategies. The study employed various algorithms, including the Floyd-Warshall algorithm, to optimize delivery routes and maximize delivery process efficiency.

Using a discrete-event simulation approach, the simulation models evening parcel delivery in a selected commuter township. Key factors include parcel distribution, route planning, driving times, customer response times, and cargo bike capacity.
